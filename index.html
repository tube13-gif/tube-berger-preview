About

The German DJ duo Tube & Berger, who first met on a school bus, have achieved an iconic (almost legendary) status, shaping the electronic music scene for nearly two decades.

"E Samba 2018" by Junior Jack & Tube & Berger plays on The Vault Radio in Grand Theft Auto VI.

They are one of the best-selling acts of all time on Beatport, and their songs have been streamed hundreds of million times. They have performed at renowned venues and festivals all over the world, including Tomorrowland, Parookaville, Pacha, Ushuaia, Hï Ibiza, Green Valley Brazil and countless other clubs and festivals.

Their musical repertoire spans a wide range, from deep and tech-house to techno, primarily released on their two labels, ZEHN and Kittball Records. In addition to their own labels, they have also delivered exceptional songs and collaborations with industry giants such as Vintage Culture, Nervo, Like Mike, Moguai, Chrissie Hynde, which have been featured on labels like FFRR, Musical Freedom, Spinnin', Toolroom, Get Physical, Universal, Virgin Records, and many more.
